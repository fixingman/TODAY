**Identity wording (Rules 35, Can 2026-11-26, from Dr Tracey Marks' "identity trap"):** labels like "you tend to…" become prescriptions, so every AI instruction that writes about Can now states the principle "describe what happened and what changed, never who the person is" (morning line ×2, About reading, Memory patterns, evening reflections, Explore; the About Today reading already said "never infer identity"). The context TODAY sends to the model (`_memoryForAI`) and its proactive observations are rewritten as observations with their basis: "Most completed tasks have landed in the afternoon", "Of the last 12 tasks added, 67% were added after 2pm", "The last 5 completed tasks took about 4 days on average", "4 tasks have been pulled back from Soon so far" (was "tends to overestimate future availability"); "Good instinct" praise removed. The Memory-patterns prompt no longer asks for "stable traits" (`semantic` now means "held across weeks"). `_observationTextIsGrounded` also rejects "you tend/usually/typically/always/never" and "good instinct". Personalization.md's Semantic/Procedural examples rewritten. New Rules 35. `insights-test.mjs` blocks trait openers in AI context/observations and checks every relevant prompt states the principle.

**Habit comeback (`habit-return`):** a new morning-pool candidate (score 90) when an active habit was checked yesterday after 2–60 missed days; evidence "Yesterday "X" was checked again after N missed days.", insight "The habit came back after a gap instead of stopping there." Only habit names and completion dates enter the pool; morning line only, 5-day cooldown, normal landed/not-landing votes and retirement apply. `sync-merge.js` pool-wording key accepts the new kind. Baseline from Can's backup: 72 restarts in 90 days. Tests: `observation-pool-test.mjs` (candidate rules, eligibility, cooldown, retirement, trait rejection).
