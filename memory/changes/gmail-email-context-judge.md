title: Email context finds the email behind any task, and shows it on phones too
about: TODAY now looks for the email behind any task you add, not only "email" or "call" tasks. When one clearly fits, ↩ shows next to the task, and Focus (or a tap on your phone) shows the one useful detail and a link to the conversation.
---
**Why:** Can's device audit (November 22) showed the old pipeline never searched: 19 of 20 logged attempts in 14 days were declined as "not communication" by the verb pre-filter plus AI classifier, 0 searches, 0 matches; the ↩ indicator was also hover-only, so phones never showed it. Design: `~/.gstack/projects/fixingman-TODAY/can-dev-design-20261122-011643.md` (office-hours + eng review + Codex outside voice, all decisions Can's).

**Search → judge (`assets/gmail.js`, `GMAIL_MATCH_POLICY` `judge-v1`):** removed the verb pre-filter, `_classifyTask` query writing, `_buildQueryFallback`, `_gmailSearch` and MiniSearch ranking (vendor files, README and `sw.js` precache line deleted). One `_lookup()` serves task-add, Focus-open, background re-check and "try other email": up to four content words → Gmail AND query (`newer_than:90d`, calendar sender excluded), widened once to the OR of the two most distinctive words while fewer than five conversations return → ≤5 conversations fetched in parallel with one shared token refresh → one candidate per conversation (subject, first and latest two non-calendar messages, metadata only) → one Claude judgement (`surface: gmail-judge`, principle-only prompt, email text is data) returning `{index|null, message, evidence, detail}` → `_ground()`: `evidence` must be an exact substring of the chosen message and every number/date in `detail` must sit inside it, plus one non-task token. No candidates, no content words or no AI key: no AI call.

**Re-check, cost and queue:** open tasks whose match is missing or expired are re-checked once a day after boot settles (`requestIdleCallback`), newest first. A per-task check record (`gmail_check_<taskId>`) stores a content-free fingerprint (policy, task text, rejected IDs, candidate thread/message IDs) so an unchanged mailbox reuses the earlier decision without an AI call; only completed checks count as "checked today". Lookups run one at a time across windows (Web Locks), task-add/Focus first, capped at 30 a day with ≤20 re-checks (`gmail_daily_v1`). "Not this thread" rejections now outlive the 24 h match for the task's life.

**Display:** ↩ is always visible at `--opacity-soft`. On touch devices (no Focus mode) it is a native 24px button (`gmail.card`, `aria-expanded`) that opens the existing Focus email card as a sibling row under the task, shaped like the inline suggestion row and re-anchored after renders; a suggestion and the card never share a task (`assistant.js`). The card shows the one-line detail; Open in Gmail opens the matched conversation (`#all/<threadId>`) instead of a search; Draft reply targets the latest message not sent by Can and is hidden when every message is Can's own.

**Diagnostics:** the existing content-free log is kept 14 days by time instead of the last 20 entries; `observationAudit()` (schema 2) adds per-day counts of lookups, matches, abstains by reason and rejections. `gmail-disconnected` is logged for every task, not only contact-verb tasks.

**Tests:** `gmail-test.mjs` rewritten around a fictional mailbox (36 checks: content words, non-communication matches, no-candidate/no-AI, abstain, AND→OR, calendar exclusion, conversation shape, evidence vs reply message, grounding near-misses, fingerprint reuse, durable rejection, queue order and caps, policy retirement, always-visible indicator, phone card, 14-day diagnostics) plus the retained ownership/race/Focus/Mail/expiry fences; `gmail-feedback-test.mjs` and `service-worker-test.mjs` (offline: content-query builder and lazy card) updated; component-contract global cap lowered to 120; CI policy adds `assistant` as a `gmail` dependent. Hand-run `scripts/gmail-judge-eval.mjs` measures the real model on fictional cases (search recall separate from judge accuracy; pass: zero wrong picks, ≥4/5 clear matches).
