| **2.81.3** | **`letgo-return` links the two events instead of counting them side by side.** Can's correction: the point was to know *which task is in the loop*, and v2.81.0 could not say — it counted let-gos and revives in the same window and, to stay honest, refused to say "of them". | **2.80.4** | **Fix: stale premises in AI context — three leaks of the same class.** Reported from production: the morning nudge said a task was "still waiting after 56 days" when it had been completed the day before.
